legacy

The City of David stands as a living bridge between past and present — a place where the stories of kings, prophets, and everyday citizens echo through ancient stones. As you walk its pathways, touch its walls, and explore its tunnels, you are not only witnessing history, but becoming part of its enduring legacy. May your visit inspire a deeper connection to the roots of Jerusalem, and a renewed appreciation for the heritage that continues to shape our future.
