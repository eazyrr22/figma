welcome to the city of david!

The City of David, located just south of Jerusalem’s Old City walls, is widely regarded as the original core of ancient Jerusalem and a site of immense archaeological and historical significance. Believed to be the location of King David’s capital around the 10th century BCE, it features layers of history spanning from the Canaanite period through the Second Temple era. Excavations have revealed monumental structures, ancient fortifications, water systems such as the famous Hezekiah’s Tunnel, and remnants of daily life from thousands of years ago. Today, the City of David functions as both an archaeological park and a living neighborhood, offering visitors a unique blend of biblical history, cultural heritage, and modern Israeli life, all within a setting overlooking the Kidron Valley and Mount of Olives.
